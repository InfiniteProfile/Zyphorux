The Zyphorux Universe

A universe built from layers of settled existence, stacked like pages and held apart by thin boundaries called Veils. Nothing in it is measured by distance. Places are measured by how deeply they sit.

How it is organized

  1. Stratum the whole universe: nine layers, Stratum 1 to 9.
  2. Realm a territory that spans one or more layers. Realm 07 is The Between.
  3. Veil a boundary dividing one Realm region from the next. There are nine in the Between.
  4. Crossing a rift made stable enough to walk through.
  5. Anchorage a settlement built on a Crossing.

Words of this universe

Veil
A boundary between regions. Each has a number; the Seventh is the thinnest that still holds.
Rift
A tear between two Veils. Most close within a Cycle. A Crossing is a rift that learned to stay.
Resonance
How strongly a thing agrees with its surroundings. High resonance means it belongs. Low means it drifts.
Echo
A remnant of an event, left in the place it happened. Echoes can be read, never changed.
Cycle
One full turn of the Between's slow pulse, the Tide. Roughly the span of a long season.
Hollow
A sealed pocket of nothing, carried by beings who cross often, so the Veils recognize them.

The Between

The Between is Realm 07, the central territory of the universe: nine Veils nested like rings, with the Seventh at its middle. It exists to connect. Every other Realm reaches its neighbors only through a Crossing here, so those who live in the Between are traders of passage, mappers, and keepers.

Veyra Crossing

The largest stable rift in the Realm, a tall ring of slow light that opens onto Strata 6, 7 and 8.

The Drift Shelves

Floating stone terraces that rise and sink with the Tide. Maps of them are only valid for one Cycle.

The Quiet Reach

A dark expanse past the Ninth Veil where Echoes gather. Few cross it. Fewer return the same.

Natural law: nothing in the Between stays put. Landmarks move with the Tide, so every inhabitant carries a Hollow, an anchor the Veils can find them by. Unique resource: Tidesilk, a thread of congealed Resonance that woven Anchorages are built from.

Zyphorux

Solitary, observant, patient. Zyphorux walks the Seventh Veil to read Echoes and rebalance Resonance where a Crossing weakens. It is spare with words and remembers every place it has stood.

Home: Hollowmere Anchorage

A settlement of Tidesilk walkways strung around a permanent rift on the edge of Veyra Crossing. Lanterns of held Resonance hang from every span, and the whole Anchorage hums in tune when the Tide turns. Zyphorux lives on the outermost ledge, where the rift is nearest, because that is where the Seventh Veil shows first. Locals leave a lit lantern on its ledge each Cycle's end, an old custom for anyone who walks the edge.

The Aurel Prism

Appearance
A slender, double-pointed crystal, always turning slowly inside three thin rings of gold light.
Origin
Formed in Cycle 1,206 when two Veils pressed together and the Echoes caught between them hardened.
Purpose
It stores memory: every Echo it touches stays readable inside it, and it will not show them to anyone it does not trust.
Location
Last set on the stone beside Zyphorux's ledge. Recently missing.
Importance
It holds the only record of the first Crossing. It is valuable for what it remembers, not for what it can do.
Bond
Only a being carrying a Hollow can hold it safely. Zyphorux has kept it since Cycle 7,300.
